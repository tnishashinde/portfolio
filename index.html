About Me

I’m a Computer Applications student with a growing interest in UX design and the ways technology shapes our everyday experiences. I’m curious about people: how they think, what influences their decisions, and how small details can change the way they interact with the world around them.

Outside academics, I’m drawn to films, storytelling, photography, and the psychology behind characters and their choices. These interests constantly influence how I observe the world, interpret experiences, and approach creative ideas.

I’m currently exploring the intersection of human behaviour, research, and interaction design, with the goal of creating digital experiences that are thoughtful, intuitive, and accessible.

Signature style

My approach to design is guided by curiosity, thoughtful composition, and attention to detail. I enjoy balancing visual expression with clarity, creating work that feels intentional while remaining functional and true to its purpose.

Inspiration

I find inspiration in films, photography, storytelling, and the psychology behind human behaviour. I’m drawn to the subtle details that shape how people think, feel, and experience the world, and I use these observations to inform my creative perspective.
